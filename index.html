How it works

When are you open?

Open Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday from 4PM, and Friday and Saturday from 12PM for lunch, until we sell out. We are closed Wednesday and Sunday. Everything is fried fresh to order, so sending your order in early is the move.

How do I place an order?

Add your items to the ticket, put your name on it, and hit "Text this order". It opens your messages with everything filled in, ready to send to (407) 850-8268. We reply to confirm.

Where do I pick up?

We'll confirm your pickup details when we reply to your order.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We deliver in Orlando and Winter Park for a flat $6 delivery fee. Outside those areas? Text us and we'll let you know. Double-check your address before sending so the driver has it right.

How do I pay?

Square, Zelle, CashApp, or cash. Just note your pick when you send the order.

Do you cater events?

Yes. Fill out the catering form and we'll send you a quote. Volume discounts run from 5% to 20% off, and a 50% non-refundable deposit locks in your date.
